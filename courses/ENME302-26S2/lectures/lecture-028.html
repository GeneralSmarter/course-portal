Key concepts

• The Laplace equation for a two-dimensional scalar field is:

\[

\frac{\partial^2 U}{\partial x^2}+\frac{\partial^2 U}{\partial y^2}=0

• Dirichlet boundary conditions prescribe the value of the dependent variable directly.

• Neumann boundary conditions prescribe a derivative or gradient.

• Ghost nodes allow a central-difference approximation to be used at a boundary where a neighbouring physical node does not exist.

Equations and definitions

• Two-dimensional Laplace equation:

\[

\frac{\partial^2 U}{\partial x^2}+\frac{\partial^2 U}{\partial y^2}=0

\]

• Second-order central difference in \(x\):

\[

\frac{\partial^2 U}{\partial x^2}\bigg|_{i,j}

\approx

\frac{U_{i+1,j}-2U_{i,j}+U_{i-1,j}}{\Delta x^2}

\]

• Second-order central difference in \(y\):

\[

\frac{\partial^2 U}{\partial y^2}\bigg|_{i,j}

\approx

\frac{U_{i,j+1}-2U_{i,j}+U_{i,j-1}}{\Delta y^2}

\]

• General uniform-grid discretisation:

\[

\frac{U_{i+1,j}-2U_{i,j}+U_{i-1,j}}{\Delta x^2}

+

\frac{U_{i,j+1}-2U_{i,j}+U_{i,j-1}}{\Delta y^2}

=0

\]

• For \(\Delta x=\Delta y=h\):

\[

U_{i+1,j}+U_{i-1,j}+U_{i,j+1}+U_{i,j-1}-4U_{i,j}=0

\]

• Equivalent interior-node update:

\[

U_{i,j}

=

\frac{1}{4}

\left(

U_{i+1,j}+U_{i-1,j}+U_{i,j+1}+U_{i,j-1}

\right)

\]

• Central approximation of a first derivative at a boundary:

\[

\frac{\partial U}{\partial x}\bigg|_{x=0}

\approx

\frac{U_{1,j}-U_{-1,j}}{2\Delta x}

\]

• Non-zero Neumann condition on the left boundary:

\[

\frac{U_{1,j}-U_{-1,j}}{2\Delta x}=1

Therefore:

\[

U_{-1,j}=U_{1,j}-2\Delta x
