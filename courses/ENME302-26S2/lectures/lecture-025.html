Key concepts

• Require significant computational resources.

• Involve licensing costs.

• An ordinary differential equation (ODE) contains total derivatives; a partial differential equation (PDE) contains partial derivatives.

• The order of a PDE is the highest order of any partial derivative in the equation.

• A homogeneous PDE has no independent source term; the terms are associated with the dependent variable.

Equations and definitions

PDE definitions

• ODE:

An equation involving ordinary or total derivatives.

• PDE:

An equation involving partial derivatives.

• PDE order:

The highest order of any partial derivative appearing in the equation.

• Homogeneous PDE:

A PDE with no independent source term, according to the lecture’s definition.

• Linear PDE:

A PDE in which the dependent variable and its derivatives occur linearly. A dependent variable or derivative raised to a power, or multiplied by another dependent-variable term, makes the equation nonlinear.
